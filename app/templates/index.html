How It Works

A streamlined three-step recovery workflow for our campus community

1. Report an Item

Quickly post details, category, and location of any lost or found item across campus facilities.

2. Claim & Verify

Browse listings and submit a claim with specific proof of ownership or verification details.

3. Reconnect

Reviewers approve verified claims, coordinating a safe handover at the campus security desk.
